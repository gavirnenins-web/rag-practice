Paris is a popular destination in France. The Eiffel Tower is one of its most recognizable landmarks.
The Louvre Museum contains famous works of art and can require several hours to explore.
The Seine River runs through Paris and boat cruises provide another way to see the city.
Tokyo is a major Japanese city. Shibuya is known for its busy crossing and shopping areas.
Asakusa is known for Senso-ji Temple and traditional streets.
Tokyo's railway and subway systems make it possible to travel around the city without a car.
